    Our story

    The Secret's in the Sauce.

    Tommy Sugo started with one pot of sauce and a stubborn idea: if the sauce is right, the rest follows.

    Roma tomatoes, confit garlic and a spice blend years in the perfecting, slow-cooked for seven hours. It goes on hand-rolled thin bases, into the shakshuka and into the bolognese. Falafel and shawarma are the same obsession, just wrapped differently.
